Privacy Policy

Effective November 21, 2026

What PaperStreet collects, how it is used, and how to request access or deletion.

Overview

PaperStreet is a Discord Activity and slash command for a simulated stock trading game. This policy explains the personal information processed when you launch the Activity or use the /portfolio command. PaperStreet is independently operated by the maintainer of the PaperStreet project, not by Discord. This notice covers PaperStreet, not Discord’s own processing.

Information we collect

  • Discord identity: your Discord user ID, display name or username, and avatar metadata when you sign in to the Activity. We use these to maintain your account and display player profiles.
  • Game records: virtual cash balance, stock holdings, order details, executed trades, predictions, points, achievements and relevant timestamps.
  • Command information: the invoking Discord user ID and interaction token are processed when you run /portfolio to retrieve your own account and send a response. We do not store the interaction token as part of your game account.
  • Technical information: requests and error diagnostics may be processed by Cloudflare, which operates the service, and by Discord. This may include IP addresses, device or browser information, and request metadata under those providers’ own policies.

PaperStreet does not require your real name, email address, bank account, payment card, or brokerage credentials. It does not read your regular Discord messages or private conversations or request access to your contact list.

How we use information

We use these records to authenticate players through Discord OAuth2; create and maintain virtual trading accounts; execute and queue simulated orders; calculate portfolios, leaderboards, predictions and achievements; fulfill /portfolio requests; secure the service; and diagnose operational errors. We do not sell personal information or use game records for targeted advertising.

Visibility to other users

Your Discord display name, avatar, virtual portfolio equity and return may appear on the in-game leaderboard and other shared game views. When you invoke /portfolio, its embed can show your virtual holdings and balance to people who can see that Discord response. Visibility depends on the channel, installation context and Discord permissions. Consider using a private conversation when you do not want others to see a command response.

Discord authentication and session tokens

PaperStreet uses Discord OAuth2 to verify your identity. Discord access tokens are used transiently to authenticate the Activity and are not stored in PaperStreet’s Cloudflare D1 database. PaperStreet issues a signed game session, held in the Activity’s browser memory, which expires after approximately one hour. Reopening or refreshing the Activity establishes a new session.

Service providers and sharing

PaperStreet uses Discord for authentication, hosting the Activity interface and command replies; Cloudflare Workers and D1 for application hosting, processing and storage; Finnhub for market quotes (stock symbol and quote requests do not intentionally include your Discord account ID); and Google Fonts for loading interface typefaces, which can involve browser requests to Google. We disclose data to these providers only as needed for the service or as required by law. We do not publish individual trade history to third-party advertisers.

Storage, security and retention

Game account and gameplay data are stored in Cloudflare D1 for as long as the account remains active, unless they are deleted following a verified request or the service is discontinued. Historic trades and order records are retained as part of the game ledger while the account exists. Operational logs and any provider-managed backups may follow Cloudflare’s own retention periods. We use signed expiring sessions, Discord-signed interaction validation, authenticated API endpoints and server-side transaction checks. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Access, correction and deletion

You can review portfolio and gameplay information inside the Activity. Your Discord display name and avatar are refreshed when you sign in again. To request a copy of your data, correction or deletion of your PaperStreet account and associated game records, open a request with the project maintainer on GitHub and indicate that you need a private way to verify your Discord account ownership. Do not put tokens, passwords or other sensitive information in a public GitHub issue. Following verification, we can delete account records and their linked holdings, trades, orders, predictions and achievements, subject to legal requirements and any provider-managed backups. Deleting or uninstalling Discord itself does not automatically erase PaperStreet’s database records.

Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, privacy law may give you rights to access, correct, erase, restrict or object to processing of personal information. Contact the project maintainer using the process above to exercise applicable rights. You may stop using PaperStreet at any time, but stopping use alone does not automatically delete stored game history.

Children

PaperStreet is intended only for people old enough to use Discord under Discord’s terms and applicable local law. It is not directed to children below the applicable minimum age, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy as the Activity changes. The effective date at the top indicates the current version. For privacy inquiries and deletion requests, contact the PaperStreet maintainer through the GitHub issue tracker. Do not include secrets in public issues. You can also review Discord’s Privacy Policy and Cloudflare’s Privacy Policy.

PaperStreet is a simulated trading game. No real money or investments are involved.
